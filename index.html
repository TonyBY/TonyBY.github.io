Biography

Yang Bai is a Research Scientist at Meta working on large-scale machine learning and decision systems for Instagram Ads. His work spans predictive modeling, causal inference, counterfactual modeling, online experimentation, and scalable ML systems, including models trained on billions of observations and deployed in production.

He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Florida, where his research focused on information retrieval, NLP, dense retrieval, and multimodal question answering. His publications include NAACL Findings, COLING, and SIGIR.
